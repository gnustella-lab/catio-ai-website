01 / FROM A THOUGHT TO SOMETHING REAL

Small questions.
Big starting points.

Catio brings everyday conversation and creative tools into one Android app. Start with a question, a rough idea or an image. Explore it in a conversation, turn it into something visual, and return to it when inspiration comes back.

01 — CONVERSATION

A place to
think out loud.

Ask for a clear explanation, brainstorm a project, work through a study topic, draft a message or refine a piece of writing. Catio is designed to help you move from “I don't know where to start” to a useful next step.

Responses support headings, lists, tables, links, quotes and code blocks, so complicated ideas are easier to follow. Copy or share a response when you want to use it elsewhere.

  • Explore an idea through follow-up questions.
  • Find past conversations with history search.
  • Continue an earlier thread instead of starting over.
  • Attach a photo to add visual context, where supported.

02 — CREATION

Give your imagination
somewhere to go.

IMAGES

Describe it. Explore it. Refine it.

Turn a written idea into an image: a character concept, an illustration, a mood for a project or a visual you have been imagining. Supported reference-image workflows give you another starting point for a new creation.

The creation screen keeps the prompt at the center and makes additional settings available when you need them. Follow progress, then find the result in your library.

VIDEO

Take an idea into motion.

Catio's video workflow turns text prompts into generated clips through its video integration. Describe a scene, its action and its atmosphere, then follow the generation as it progresses.

Video is a credit-based feature for eligible paid plans. Availability, duration and generation options depend on the release configuration and provider support. Video is not included in the free plan.

A home for the things you make.The library brings your image and video creations together, while conversation history keeps your thoughts easy to revisit.

03 — PERSONALITY

Same little cat.
Your kind of energy.

Choose the companion that fits how you like to think. Five personalities change Catio's look and conversational style. All five are available across every plan, and you can change your choice later.

GET TO KNOW YOUR CATIO

Warm

Patient, encouraging and welcoming. A little support when you want to take things one step at a time.

A personality preview, not a live AI chat.

A companion with a sense of progress. Levels, XP and achievements give your Catio journey its own playful rhythm.

04 — CONTINUITY & CONTROL

A fresh conversation.
A familiar companion.

Optional personal memory helps Catio carry useful context between conversations. You decide whether to use it, and you can manage what is remembered. Your personality choice works even when memory is turned off.

Privacy controls include data export and account deletion. Catio keeps provider credentials on the server, and account access uses Google sign-in. The introductory tour and personalization come before sign-in, so you can meet your companion first.

05 / A CLOSER LOOK

Built around
your everyday possibilities.

Learning & understanding

Break down difficult topics, ask for another explanation, compare concepts, and organize notes into clear steps. Catio can support your learning; check important facts against reliable sources.

Writing & communication

Develop a first draft, change its tone, simplify a paragraph or explore several ways to express the same idea. Conversation makes revision an ongoing process.

Ideas & creative projects

Explore names, stories, visual concepts and project directions. Move between written exploration and media creation without losing the thread of the original idea.

An experience that adapts

Light and dark themes, reduced-motion preferences, English and Brazilian Portuguese interface text, and a personalized companion make the app feel more comfortable to use.

Clear usage information

Your profile shows your current plan, usage and renewal timing. Catio is being developed with a free tier and Mini, Super, Mega and Ultra options for different levels of use.

Room to grow

Higher tiers expand conversation and creative allowances. Final launch pricing, limits, supported models and purchasing options will be confirmed when the public release is ready.

06 — THE NEXT CHAPTER

Curiosity doesn't
stop here.

Catio's longer-term direction includes richer ways to connect useful information and creative tools. Ideas under consideration include web-assisted answers, a structured recipe experience with source links and visual references, and voice interactions.

These are future possibilities, not confirmed launch features. Development priorities and availability may change.

BEFORE WE MEET

A few things to know.

When will Catio AI be available?

Catio is in active development. A public release date has not been announced. This site introduces the project and the experience being built.

Which platforms are planned?

The current app is a native Android project. Store distribution is being prepared; listings and regional availability will be announced when ready. Other platforms have not been confirmed.

Will there be a free plan?

The project includes a free tier for conversations and a limited image allowance, alongside paid tiers with larger allowances. Video requires an eligible paid plan. Final release terms will be confirmed at launch.

Are all features ready for public use?

The project already contains conversation, personality, memory, media and account workflows. Store preparation, provider availability and release validation are ongoing. Development previews are not a promise that every feature will be available in every region or plan at launch.

How much should I rely on an AI answer?

AI can make mistakes. Use Catio as a thinking and creative companion, and verify important information. It does not replace qualified medical, legal or financial advice.
